# 15 — Fiber Connectors

> 📚 **Course:** CompTIA Network+ (N10-009)
> 
> 📅 **Last modified:** 2026-08-02

---

When working with fiber optics, it is essential to identify and use the correct connector type for each specific connection, as there are multiple varieties.

## 1. SC Connector (Subscriber Connector)

The **SC** connector, also known as the _Subscriber Connector_, is one of the most common types.

- **Physical characteristics:** It is often referred to as a **"Square Connector"** due to its shape, or sometimes as a **"Standard Connector"**.
- **Locking mechanism:** It uses a **push-in** system that clicks into place; to remove it, you must pull the connector to release it.
- **Usage:** It is very common in **data centers**. Although they handle single fibers, they are often paired (one for transmit and one for receive).

## 2. LC Connector (Local Connector)

The **LC** or _Local Connector_ is another extremely widespread option, characterized by its small size.

- **Nomenclature:** It may also be referred to as a **"Lucent Connector"** or **"Little Connector"**.
- **Locking mechanism:** It features a **clip on the top** similar to that of Ethernet cables; to extract it, you must press the clip downward.
- **Advantages:** Being smaller than the SC, it allows for higher connection density and is commonly used in **duplex pairs** to handle both transmission and reception simultaneously.

## 3. ST Connector (Straight Tip)

The **ST** (_Straight Tip_) connector is distinguished by its particular latching system.

- **Bayonet mechanism:** It uses a bayonet mount; you insert it into the interface and give it a **slight twist** to lock it securely.
- **Security:** This system prevents the cable from being accidentally disconnected, a common occurrence when working in densely cabled racks.
- **Structure:** It includes a **protective ferrule** around the fiber to ensure its integrity.

## 4. MPO Connector (Multifiber Push-on)

To address the problem of the physical bulk of single connectors and increase efficiency, the **MPO** connector was introduced.

- **High density:** A single MPO connector can house **12 individual fibers** within a single cable.
- **Alternative names:** It is often called **MTP**, a registered trade name of Corning.
- **Mechanism:** Similar to the SC connector, it pushes in and requires a slight pull to be released.
